LEGITIMATE-INTEREST ASSESSMENT - PURPOSE CONFIRMED, REASONED REVIEW
1. Purpose
Protect a public enquiry endpoint and its finite delivery capacity against
plausible automated misuse and excessive requests. This is a specific, present
operational interest. Preventive protection does not require waiting for damage;
"just in case" alone would not explain an unlimited collection programme.

2. Necessity and alternatives
Edge limits bound bursts before expensive work; D1 supplies durable quotas;
Turnstile checks whether submissions are automated. These address different
failure modes. IP limits alone can be evaded through multiple addresses, while a
challenge alone does not impose an email-volume cap. This is an architectural
rationale, not measured evidence that every threshold is optimally calibrated.
Validation limits malformed input but does not distinguish valid automated spam.
Honeypots may catch simple bots but can be bypassed and can affect autofill.
Direct-email-only contact avoids the website challenge but removes the structured
form and shifts abuse handling to the mailbox. It remains a useful alternative
for affected visitors, not proof that the form's processing is necessary.
The less intrusive alternative has now been implemented: initiate the challenge
on form interaction, avoiding third-party Turnstile processing for visitors who
only read the page while retaining server-side token verification. Focus includes
keyboard navigation, pointerdown covers mouse/pen/touch, and input covers editing.
Merely focusing the form can still start verification without eventual submission;
that is a deliberate boundary tied to use of the protected form, rather than all
homepage visits. It allows verification during composition and keeps the same
verification gate. No performance or abuse-rate measurements are asserted.

3. Balancing and safeguards
Visitors can reasonably expect protection of an enquiry form. The deferred loader
now avoids Turnstile signal processing just to read the homepage. Shared IPs, VPNs,
assistive technology, script restrictions, errors and retries can affect access.
Five attempts per 10 minutes can encompass legitimate users sharing an address;
the global daily cap can affect everyone. Review complaints/rejection patterns
without introducing unnecessary message logging. No excessive rejection rate has
been demonstrated, so do not claim one or require a fabricated abuse study.
Counter minimisation, daily-changing identifiers, finite windows, bounded cleanup,
clear retry messages and direct email reduce impact. Raw IP processing at the
edge and provider records still exist. Direct email mitigates exclusion but does
not undo processing already triggered by interacting with the form.

Assessment: a reasonable proportionality case exists for bounded rate limiting
and verification of form use. No evidence shows those controls inherently excessive.
The identified pre-interaction scope concern is resolved in this release.
The preventive purpose is already confirmed. Monitor shared-IP/false-positive impact
and cleanup backlog as routine operational follow-up. This assessment does not
approve unrelated analytics under a security interest, or approve Cloudflare's
independent-controller purpose on Cloudflare's behalf.

TURNSTILE STORAGE - SEPARATE ANALYSIS
Cloudflare's official Privacy Addendum describes its bot-detection signals as
necessary for security. It distinguishes processing on the customer's behalf from
Cloudflare's own controller purpose of improving detection. This supports the
security explanation; it does not specify cf.turnstile.u's exact lifetime.
AKI guidance distinguishes necessary site-function storage from additional storage
requiring prior informed consent. It does not name or approve cf.turnstile.u.
Taken together, these sources support evaluating the observed storage as functional
security storage, not automatically treating it as advertising or analytics.
They do not establish a blanket exemption for every Turnstile use or its timing.
GDPR legitimate interest and the browser-storage consent/exemption question are
separate. Necessary-for-provider-service language is not automatically a finding
that every storage operation is strictly necessary for this visitor's requested
service. Deferring loading to form use improves that connection, but does not
by itself constitute consent or conclusively establish a storage exemption.

The entry was observed under challenges.cloudflare.com. Its precise lifetime
remains unverified; do not substitute verification-token validity, claim session
expiry, or promise deletion on browser close. The notice identifies the entry,
provider, verification purpose, provider documentation and browser removal controls.
This qualified account is preferable to a fabricated duration. A short clarifying
sentence that the exact provider-controlled lifetime has not been established is
an optional transparency improvement, not implemented here. The uncertainty alone
is not proof consent is required; nor does qualified wording replace any applicable
storage disclosure/consent requirement. Seek provider-specific clarification when
available, without making an unanswered lifetime query an indefinite blanket bar
to publishing the rest of the accurate notice.
